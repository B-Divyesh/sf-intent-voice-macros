Privacy in plain words

Your commands, activity log, and license token stay on your device unless you choose a billing action.

What the extension stores

The extension stores approved commands and the latest 100 command results in browser local storage.

If you buy or restore a license, it also stores the token and its latest verification result.

How speech works

The extension asks the browser to process one phrase after you press the talk button.

It requests local speech processing when the browser supports it. Browser and operating-system support varies.

The extension does not record or store audio.

What leaves your device

A license check leaves your device only after you provide a license.

The request contains the token and goes to the Sociobot billing API.

Your browser’s speech service may send audio when local processing is unavailable. Its privacy policy applies.

Sociobot/Dodo handles checkout and refunds as the merchant of record.

Website data

A command inspects only the selector you approved on the active page.

The extension does not crawl pages, collect page content, or run in the background.

This site uses no cookies, analytics, ad trackers, or third-party scripts.

Your controls

Delete commands, clear the log, export your setup, or remove the extension to erase its browser storage.

Clear this site’s storage to remove a license saved on the website.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the public source repository (external).

Run approved browser actions by voice or typing.
