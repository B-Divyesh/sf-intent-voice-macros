Privacy, in plain words.

Your commands, activity log, and license token are stored on your device. Say the Action does not collect audio or behavioral analytics.

What the extension stores

The extension stores approved command cards, up to 100 recent command outcomes, and—if you buy or restore the unlock—a license token and its last verification result in browser local storage. Audio is sent only to the speech-recognition facility provided by your browser or operating system; the extension never records or stores audio.

What leaves your device

Only a license verification request leaves your device when you provide a license. It contains the license token and goes to the Sociobot billing API. Checkout is operated by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record, under their own transaction records and legal obligations.

Website data

When you run a command, the extension inspects only the selector you approved on the active page. It does not crawl pages, collect page content, or operate in the background. This marketing site uses no cookies, ad trackers, third-party scripts, or analytics.

Your controls

Delete command cards individually, clear the local log, export your setup, or remove the extension to erase its browser storage. Clear site storage to remove a license saved on this website.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the public source repository.
